Things that can be done to you

These are the basic building blocks of conditions.

  • Compelled: limited to free actions and one standard action per turn, chosen by another, controlling character. superseded by Controlled.
  • Controlled: The character's actions each turn are dictated by another, controlling character
  • Dazed: limited to free actions and a single standard action, that action may be used to move as usual. Superseded by Stunned
  • Debilitated: happens when a character has one or more abilities lowered below -5. see the debilitated table below
  • Defenseless: Active defense of 0. allows for the Finishing Blow maneuver
  • Disabled: -5 circumstance penalty on checks. If the penalty is for a specific check it is added to the name. Superseded by Debilitated if it applies to the same ability
  • Fatigued: same as hindered, but characters recover after an hour of rest
  • Hindered: -1 speed rank (move at half speed). superseded by Immobile
  • Immobile: no movement speed and cannot move from the spot they occupy. Immobile does not affect their ability to take actions
  • Impaired: -2 circumstance penalty on checks. If the penalty is for a specific check it is added to the name superseded by Disabled
  • Normal: The character is unaffected by other conditions at this time, acting normally
  • Stunned: Cannot take any actions, even free actions
  • Transformed: Some or all traits altered by an outside agency. Effects can vary greatly however the character's character point total does not increase.
  • Unaware: Unable to make interaction or Perception checks or any action based on them. If the penalty is for a specific check it is added to the name others have full concealment from all of a character's unaware senses
  • Vulnerable: Active defenses are halved (rounding up). Superseded by Defenseless
  • Weakened: Temporary loss of character points in a trait. Superseded by Debilitated

Debilitated

Debilitated is the lowest that ability ranks can get, once debilitated they cannot lower any further

Ability below -5 Effect
Strength, Agility, or Dexterity Target is Defenseless, Immobilized, and Stunned
Stamina Target is dying and has a -5 to Fortitude checks to avoid death
Fighting Target is Dazed, Defenseless, and cannot make close attacks
Intellect, Awareness, or Presence Target is Unaware and remains as such until restored to at least a -5 rank

Combined conditions are, as the name implies, several basic conditions bundled under a single descriptor
The individual conditions that make up a combined condition can be removed or superseded with individually.

  • Asleep: Defenseless, Stunned, and Unaware.
    removed by a hearing perception check over 3 DoS, any sudden movement, or any effect that allows a resistance check.
  • Blind: Hindered, Visually Unaware, and Vulnerable. Also may be Impaired or Disabled for activities involving vision
  • Bound: Defenseless, Immobile, and Impaired
  • Deaf: Allows for Surprise Attacks, interaction is limited to sign-language and lip-reading
  • Dying: Incapacitated and near death. When this condition is gained make a DC 15 Fort check. 1 DoS nothing happens, 2 Dos character stabilizes, Fail character remains dying. 3 total DoF means death. Make fortitude checks until dead or stabilized
  • Entranced:Stunned, can only pay attention to the entrancing effect.
    removed by any obvious threat, or ally interaction check of DC 10 + effect rank
  • Exhausted: Impaired and Hindered.
    recover after an hour of rest in comfort
  • Incapacitated: Defenseless, Stunned, and Unaware. Also fall Prone unless there is outside support
  • Paralyzed: Defenseless, Immobile, Physically Stunned. unable to move by still aware and able to do purely mental actions
  • Prone: Hindered. -5 circumstance penalty on close attack checks. Opponents gain a +5 bonus to close attack checks but a -5 penalty to ranged attacks
  • Restrained: Hindered and Vulnerable. If the restraints are anchored to a immovable object or character you are Immobile instead of Hindered
  • Staggered: Dazed and Hindered
  • Suprised: Stunned and Vulnerable
